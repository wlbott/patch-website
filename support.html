Common questions

How do I join the beta?

Patch is currently in beta through Apple TestFlight. Install TestFlight from the App Store, then tap here to join the Patch beta. If the link says the beta is full, email us and we'll add you to the list.

I'm not receiving my verification code

Patch signs you in with your phone number and a 6-digit text message code. If it doesn't arrive:

  • Check that the number you entered is correct, including the country code.
  • Wait 60 seconds and request a new code — carrier delivery can lag.
  • Make sure you aren't blocking messages from unknown senders.
  • Still stuck? Email us with the number you're trying to use and we'll look into it.

Who can see my check-ins?

Only the small group of people you've added to your patch. There is no public feed, no followers, and no algorithm. See our Privacy Policy for the full detail on what we collect and why.

How do I add or remove someone from my patch?

Open the Friends screen in the app to invite someone by contact or remove them. Removing someone immediately stops them from seeing any of your future check-ins.

How do I turn notifications off?

Go to Settings on your iPhone, find Patch in the app list, then tap Notifications and turn off Allow Notifications. You can keep using Patch with notifications off.

How do I delete my account and my data?

You can request full deletion at any time. Email will@yourpatchapp.com from the address associated with your account, or with the phone number you signed up with, and ask us to delete your account. We remove your profile, check-ins, and associated data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Does Patch use my health data?

Only if you opt in to Bionic Bunny. If you do, Patch reads heart rate variability, resting heart rate, and sleep hours from Apple Health so it can privately let you know when your metrics look unusual. That data is never shared with your patch or with anyone else, and you can turn it off in the app at any time.

Is Patch free?

Yes. Patch is free during the beta, with no ads and nothing to buy.

If you're in crisis

Patch is a tool for staying connected with friends. It is not a crisis service and is not a substitute for professional care.

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, call 911.

For free, confidential support 24/7, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or text HOME to 741741 to reach the Crisis Text Line.
